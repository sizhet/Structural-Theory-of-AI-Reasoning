## [1.0.0] — 2026-08-27

### Initial Public Release

Version **1.0.0** establishes the first complete release of the **Structural Theory of AI Reasoning (STAR)**.

STAR develops a structural interpretation of AI reasoning that connects:

* answer generation,
* structural growth,
* structural localization,
* LLM reasoning,
* folding and unfolding,
* Structural Intelligence,
* reasoning–structure feedback,
* and future structural AI architectures.

The central shift is from viewing reasoning only as:

```text
Question
    ↓
Computation
    ↓
Answer
```

toward viewing reasoning as a process involving:

```text
Structure
    ↓
Localization
    ↓
Unfolding
    ↓
Reasoning
    ↓
Candidate Structural Growth
    ↓
Validation
    ↓
Updated Structure
```

The release establishes the conceptual progression:

```text
Answer
    ↓
Structural Growth
    ↓
Structural Reasoning
    ↓
LLM Structural Interpretation
    ↓
Folding / Unfolding
    ↓
Unfolding / Localization
    ↓
Living Reasoning
    ↓
Future AI Reasoning Architecture
```

## Added

### Core STAR Research Series

Added the eight-document STAR research sequence:

1. **STAR-001 — What Is AI Reasoning?**

   * Reframes reasoning beyond answer production.
   * Introduces structural change as an important dimension of reasoning.
   * Distinguishes output generation from persistent intelligence growth.

2. **STAR-002 — From Answers to Structural Growth**

   * Develops the transition from answer-centric reasoning to structure-centric reasoning.
   * Introduces candidate structural delta generation.
   * Separates generated structure from validated persistent structure.

3. **STAR-003 — How Structural Intelligence Reasons**

   * Develops a structural reasoning process based on localization, computation, traversal, gap detection, and structural bridging.
   * Connects reasoning to explicit structural operations.

4. **STAR-004 — How Can an LLM Reason?**

   * Examines LLM reasoning from a structural perspective.
   * Moves beyond a purely Winner-Take-All interpretation of token generation.
   * Introduces the possibility of implicit or latent structural reasoning.

5. **STAR-005 — LLM Folding and Unfolding**

   * Introduces Folding and Unfolding as a structural interpretation of LLM training and inference.
   * Describes learned intelligence as potentially folded into distributed internal representations.
   * Interprets generation and reasoning as selective structural unfolding.

6. **STAR-006 — Unfolding Meets Localization**

   * Connects LLM Unfolding with Structural Intelligence Localization.
   * Proposes an operational correspondence between unfolded LLM reasoning regions and localized SI reasoning regions.
   * Establishes a conceptual bridge between LLM reasoning and Structural Intelligence.

7. **STAR-007 — The Living Reasoning Loop**

   * Extends reasoning from a one-way computation into a feedback process.
   * Connects reasoning, candidate structural delta, validation, and structural update.
   * Introduces the concept of a Living Reasoning system.

8. **STAR-008 — Future AI Reasoning Architectures**

   * Extends the STAR framework toward future AI system architecture.
   * Explores division of labor between model intelligence and structural runtime intelligence.
   * Introduces possible future directions including explicit structural runtimes and Unfolding-oriented architectures.

## Added — Core Structural Equations

Established a set of recurring conceptual equations across the STAR series.

### Reasoning Beyond Static Output

```math
Reasoning
\neq
Answer\ Generation\ Alone
```

### Structural Growth

```math
S(t)
\rightarrow
R(t)
\rightarrow
\Delta S(t)
\rightarrow
S(t+1)
```

where:

* `S(t)` represents the available structural state,
* `R(t)` represents reasoning,
* `ΔS(t)` represents candidate structural change,
* `S(t+1)` represents an updated structural state after appropriate validation.

### LLM Folding and Unfolding

```math
Observed\ Expression
\rightarrow
Folded\ Internal\ Representation
```

and:

```math
Activated\ Internal\ Structure
\rightarrow
Selective\ Unfolding
\rightarrow
Explicit\ Expression
```

### Unfolding and Localization

Introduced the operational comparison:

```math
R_{LLM}^{Unfold}
\approx
R_{SI}^{Localized}
```

where `≈` denotes a possible correspondence in operational reasoning region rather than identity of internal mechanisms.

## Added — Structural Growth Boundary

Established an explicit distinction between:

```math
Generated\ Structure
```

and:

```math
Validated\ Persistent\ Structure
```

Therefore:

```math
Generated\ Structure
\neq
Validated\ Structure
```

A candidate structural delta does not automatically become persistent intelligence.

Validation, governance, evidence, or other acceptance mechanisms may be required before structural persistence.

---

## Added — Folding / Unfolding Interpretation

Introduced a structural interpretation in which:

```text
Training / Learning
        ↓
Distributed Structural Folding

Inference / Generation
        ↓
Selective Structural Unfolding
```

This interpretation provides a possible conceptual bridge between:

* distributed neural representation,
* LLM generation,
* reasoning,
* structural localization,
* and future structural runtime systems.

The release explicitly treats Folding and Unfolding as a **structural interpretation**, not as a claim that Transformer inference literally performs the mathematical inverse of Transformer training.

---

## Added — LLM / Structural Intelligence Bridge

Established a conceptual bridge between two reasoning paradigms:

```text
LLM
    ↓
Implicit / Distributed Structure
    ↓
Selective Unfolding
```

and:

```text
Structural Intelligence
    ↓
Explicit / Operable Structure
    ↓
Localization
```

The STAR framework proposes that these approaches may converge operationally on related reasoning regions while remaining different in internal representation and mechanism.

## Research Boundaries

Version 1.0.0 explicitly distinguishes between:

```text
Observed AI behavior
        ↓
Structural interpretation
        ↓
Research hypothesis
        ↓
Future architecture
```

In particular, this release does **not** claim that:

* current LLMs contain literal symbolic reasoning graphs corresponding to STAR diagrams;
* Transformer inference literally reverses Transformer training;
* Unfolding is an already standardized Transformer operation;
* LLM Unfolding and SI Localization use identical internal mechanisms;
* candidate structural growth automatically becomes persistent knowledge;
* the complete Living Reasoning Loop is already implemented in current LLM systems;
* future Unfolding-oriented architectures described by STAR already exist as established architectures.

These distinctions are part of the intended scientific scope of STAR v1.0.0.
